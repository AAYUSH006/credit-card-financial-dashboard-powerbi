# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

# Credit Card Financial Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Credit Card Financial Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze customer demographics, transaction behavior, and financial performance for a credit card business.

The dashboard enables business users to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify revenue-driving customer segments, analyze transaction trends, and support data-driven strategic decision-making.

The project combines customer profile data with transaction-level financial data to provide a comprehensive view of customer behavior and business performance.

---

# Business Problem

Financial institutions generate massive volumes of customer and transaction data every day. However, raw data alone does not provide meaningful business insights.

Business teams often face challenges such as:

- Identifying high-value customer segments.
- Understanding spending behavior across different customer groups.
- Monitoring quarterly revenue trends.
- Evaluating credit card performance.
- Tracking customer acquisition costs.
- Measuring customer satisfaction.
- Identifying opportunities to increase profitability.

Without an interactive analytical solution, these insights are difficult to obtain efficiently.

This dashboard addresses these challenges by transforming raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence.

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard monitors the following KPIs:

- Total Revenue
- Total Interest Earned
- Total Income
- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSS)
- Total Transaction Amount
- Total Transaction Count
- Customer Acquisition Cost
- Annual Fees
- Credit Utilization Ratio

# Business Questions Addressed

The dashboard is designed to answer key business questions such as:

### Customer Analysis

- Which customer age group generates the highest revenue?
- Which occupations contribute the most revenue?
- How does revenue vary by gender?
- Which education level contributes the highest revenue?
- What is the impact of marital status on revenue generation?
- Which salary group is the most profitable?
- Which states generate the highest revenue?

---

### Transaction Analysis

- Which expenditure category contributes the highest revenue?
- Which card category performs best?
- Which transaction method (Chip, Swipe, Online) is most commonly used?
- How many transactions occur each quarter?
- How does revenue change across quarters?
- Which quarter generates the highest revenue?

---

### Financial Analysis

- What is the total revenue generated?
- How much interest income is earned?
- How much annual fee revenue is collected?
- What is the customer acquisition cost by card category?
- What is the average customer credit utilization?

---

### Customer Segmentation

- Which customer groups are most valuable?
- Which demographic segments should be targeted for marketing campaigns?
- Which customer segments require better engagement?
