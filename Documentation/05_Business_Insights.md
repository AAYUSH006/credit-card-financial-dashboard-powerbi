# Overview

The **Credit Card Financial Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of customer demographics, spending behavior, transaction patterns, and financial performance.

By integrating customer profile data with transaction-level information, the dashboard enables stakeholders to identify high-value customer segments, monitor financial performance, evaluate transaction behavior, and make informed business decisions.

The analysis combines customer analytics, financial KPIs, transaction trends, customer acquisition metrics, and geographic performance into an interactive Business Intelligence solution.

---

# Dashboard Snapshot

> ## 📸 Dashboard Snapshot
>
> The project consists of two interactive Power BI reports:
>
> - 👥 **Customer Report** – Customer demographics, segmentation, customer satisfaction, and revenue analysis.
> - 💳 **Transaction Report** – Financial KPIs, spending behavior, transaction analysis, and card performance.
>
> Together, these reports provide a **360° view of customer performance and overall business operations**, enabling executives to monitor KPIs and explore business insights interactively.

# Customer Insights

## Revenue by Gender

### Observation

- Male customers generated approximately **31M** in revenue.
- Female customers generated approximately **26M** in revenue.

> **📌 Key Finding**
>
> Male customers contribute slightly more revenue than female customers, although both customer groups remain significant contributors to overall business performance.

### Business Insight

Marketing campaigns should continue targeting both segments while exploring opportunities to increase engagement among female customers.

---

## Revenue by Age Group

### Observation

Customers aged **40–50 years** contribute the highest revenue, followed by customers aged **50–60 years**.

> **🎯 Customer Segment**
>
> Customers aged **40–50 years** represent the bank's most valuable customer segment and are ideal candidates for premium products and loyalty programs.

### Business Insight

Middle-aged customers demonstrate higher purchasing power and greater credit card utilization.

---

## Revenue by Income Group

### Observation

The **High Income** customer segment contributes the largest share of overall revenue.

> **💰 Revenue Driver**
>
> Higher-income customers consistently generate the greatest business value, reinforcing the importance of retaining premium customer segments.

### Business Insight

Income level has a strong positive relationship with spending behavior and profitability.

---

## Revenue by Occupation

### Observation

Self-employed professionals and Businessmen generate the highest revenue among all occupations.

> **📈 Business Opportunity**
>
> These customer groups present excellent opportunities for premium credit cards, business banking services, and personalized financial products.

### Business Insight

Profession-based customer segmentation can support targeted marketing strategies.

---

## Revenue by Education

### Observation

Graduate customers contribute the highest overall revenue.

### Business Insight

Customers with higher education levels appear to demonstrate stronger purchasing behavior and greater engagement with credit card products.

---

## Revenue by Marital Status

### Observation

Married customers contribute slightly more revenue than Single customers.

### Business Insight

Married customers represent a stable and profitable customer segment suitable for long-term relationship programs.

---

## Revenue by State

### Observation

Texas (TX), New York (NY), and California (CA) together contribute approximately **68.8%** of total revenue.

> **🌎 Geographic Insight**
>
> Nearly **69%** of overall business revenue originates from just three states, highlighting strong regional concentration.

### Business Insight

These regions should remain priority markets for customer acquisition and retention initiatives.

# Transaction Insights

## Quarterly Revenue Performance

### Observation

Revenue remained relatively consistent throughout the year, with **Q3** recording the highest quarterly revenue.

> **📊 Seasonal Trend**
>
> Q3 represents the strongest performing quarter, suggesting increased seasonal spending activity.

### Business Insight

Understanding seasonal spending patterns can help optimize promotional campaigns and marketing investments.

---

## Transaction Amount & Count

### Observation

Transaction amount increased by **35%**, while transaction count increased by **3.4%** compared to the previous week.

> **📈 Customer Spending Pattern**
>
> Customers spent significantly more per transaction rather than simply performing more transactions.

### Business Insight

Higher average transaction values contributed substantially to revenue growth.

---

## Revenue by Card Category

### Observation

Blue and Silver cards account for approximately **93.5%** of all transactions.

> **💳 Portfolio Insight**
>
> The business is heavily dependent on mass-market credit card products.

### Business Insight

Increasing adoption of Gold and Platinum cards presents an opportunity to improve customer lifetime value and profitability.

---

## Revenue by Expenditure Type

### Observation

The highest spending categories include:

- Bills
- Entertainment
- Fuel
- Grocery

Travel contributes comparatively lower spending.

> **🛒 Spending Behavior**
>
> Customers primarily rely on credit cards for recurring household expenses and everyday purchases.

### Business Insight

Everyday spending categories remain the primary drivers of transaction revenue.

---

## Revenue by Transaction Mode

### Observation

Swipe transactions generate the highest revenue, followed by Chip and Online transactions.

> **💡 Payment Preference**
>
> Swipe remains the preferred payment method, although digital payment adoption continues to increase.

### Business Insight

Payment channel trends can help prioritize future payment innovations and customer experience improvements.
